POKERSOC BRAND ASSETS
=====================

Place the club logos here:

  pokersoc_black_icon.png   window and taskbar icon
  pokersoc_white_icon.png   white mark for dark backgrounds
  5.png                     tournament TV board watermark

Recommendations:
- PNG with transparent background
- At least 800px on the long edge
- Works best as a single mark / wordmark (not a full poster)

The board loads 5.png at 50% opacity, rotated diagonally
across the lower-right of the screen (roughly 6 o'clock → 3 o'clock).

After adding or replacing a file, rebuild so it is included in the program.
