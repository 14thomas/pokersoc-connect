POKERSOC BRAND ASSETS
=====================

Place the club logo here for the tournament TV board watermark:

  5.png

Recommendations:
- PNG with transparent background
- At least 800px on the long edge
- Works best as a single mark / wordmark (not a full poster)

The board loads this file at 50% opacity, rotated diagonally
across the lower-right of the screen (roughly 6 o'clock → 3 o'clock).

After adding or replacing the file, rebuild or re-run so it is
copied into build-gui/Assets/Brand/.
